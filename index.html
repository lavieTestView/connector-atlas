CONNECTOR ATLAS

Find a connector by part number or by what the harness needs. Pictures are Visio-ready.

Connector data comes from catalog.js and pictures from the img folder next to this page. Front views are cut from the Amphenol D-sub and MIL-DTL-38999 catalogs (pin insert front; socket faces mirrored with readable designators). Side views: engineer's D-sub pictures, the W114 D38999 plug master, the Amphenol 38999 catalog receptacle drawings, and the supplied BNC drawing. Fields marked “verify” come from rules or catalogs that were not confirmed against a live distributor page. Distributor links open a search on that site.
